Reverse Engineering Wiki

This place is meant to be a goto knowledge-base for reverse engineering information, cheatsheet and techniques. It compiles all the interesting information I could find when walking down the path of the reverse engineer journey.

The content focuses on concise, simple and digest information bits about various topics: tooling, reversing techniques, debugging, anticheat, etc. The goal is to provide an easy-to-look place for quick info retrieval, not a training.

This project is a work-in-progress and will be for a while, I take notes on the fly when I face something interesting, for the moment only the ghidra and arm architecture topics are sligthly covered. More to come.

Hacking is sharing, therefore open-source tools, systems, books, articles and so on, will always be prioritized here over any commercial product. Also, I like Ghidra.

Project's Philosophy

Contribution are always welcome, try to keep things concise and use the most simple wording possible, when possible. Follow this and your contribution will likely be merged into the wiki.

  • Open source is not about you. - And this project is certainly not. I do things the way I like for myself and the people I like first.
  • NO AI - No AI techniques, tooling, no LLM generated content. I prefer a few typo in your contribution than slop.
